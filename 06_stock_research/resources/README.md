## What this folder is

A worked example of building a view on a **commodity producer**, where the earnings driver is a metal price you cannot forecast and the company's "balance sheet" is its reserve base.

The note applies:

1. **A demand-framework reading of the mining business** — reserves and grades as the real asset, cost curve position as the moat, mine life and permit expiry as the risk.
2. **A scenario-based valuation** (profitability scenarios, main-business P/E first, segment SOTP as a cross-check) — every metal price is labelled a *scenario assumption*, never a forecast.
3. **An explicit data anchor** — 2025 annual report, 2026 interim report, A/H quotes, LME gold and copper spot.

## Valuation approach in one line

FY2026E net profit ≈ RMB 81–85bn (taking 83bn), EPS ≈ ¥3.15, ≈ **9.4x** at the research-date price; scenarios then flex gold/copper prices and assign a through-cycle multiple to get target market caps and implied A/H prices.

## The annual-report reading map

The most reusable artifact here is how to read a 352-page mining annual report efficiently:

| Pages | Section | What to extract |
|---|---|---|
| p17–24 | Resources and reserves | The "balance sheet" of a miner: total table, per-mine tables with equity share, grade, remaining life, permit expiry |
| p25–33 | Operating products | One booklet per metal (gold / copper / lithium / zinc-lead / silver-moly / iron-cobalt): output, cost, expansion progress |
| p34–54 | MD&A | Operating narrative, M&A history |
| p55–61 | Next-year plan and outlook | The most forward-looking section: industry view, output guidance, risk disclosures |

This map is in `README.zh.md`.
